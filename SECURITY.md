# Security

`sello` is small and unaudited. If you find a way to:
- forge a signature that verifies,
- make the log accept a backdated or altered entry,
- leak a private key into the public directory,
- or break anything else it claims,

please open an issue marked **security** (no secrets in it), or write to
izzycognita@gmail.com. I'll say what I changed and credit you, if you want the credit.

What `sello` does not try to defend against is listed in the README, under
"what it doesn't prove." Those limits are by design, not bugs.
